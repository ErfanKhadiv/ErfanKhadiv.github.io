FLAGSHIP RESEARCH · SOLE-AUTHORED · 2026

HBALS v2

A hybrid Bees Algorithm with 2-opt local search for the Travelling Salesman Problem. The v2 study compares six methods under equal move-evaluation budgets, evaluates seven ablation variants, applies non-parametric statistical tests and includes run-by-run verification. Across 12 main TSPLIB instances, HBALS achieved the best average rank (1.25) and a mean gap of 0.299%; its overall difference from ILS-2opt was not statistically significant.

Experience

A summer at the port

IT and Networking Intern · Ports & Maritime Organization of Iran, Astara · Summer 2025 · 120 hours

Before this internship, networks were something I drew on paper and configured in lab exercises. At the port in Astara they were cabinets, cables and people who needed everything to just work.

I got to work on real equipment: switches and patch panels, VLANs, VPN, DNS and DHCP. I learned that troubleshooting is mostly patience and knowing the next good question to ask, and I enjoyed it far more than I expected.

Working in a port was a special experience. It is a place that keeps moving, and the network quietly holds a lot of that together. It made me care about systems that people depend on without ever thinking about them.
